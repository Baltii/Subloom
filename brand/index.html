Clear, never grandiose

Say “See your upcoming renewals.” Leave promises of financial freedom, guaranteed savings, or automatic cancellation behind.

Friendly, never fussy

Give each section a purpose. Use rounded surfaces, generous space and one main illustration. The logo itself stays flat.

One mark, every time
